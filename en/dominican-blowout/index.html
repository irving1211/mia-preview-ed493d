Everything you may want to know before coming in.

How long does a Dominican blowout take?

It depends on how long and thick your hair is: long, full hair takes more time. For a shorter wait, come Tuesday to Thursday.

How long does a Dominican blowout last?

With basic care, like a night wrap and keeping it dry, many clients still feel good about it all week, and some stretch it to 10 days.

Does a Dominican blowout damage your hair?

It uses heat, so what matters is good technique and not doing it more often than your hair can handle. If your hair is weak or color-treated, tell us and we will guide you.

Does a Dominican blowout work on very curly or 4c hair?

Yes, we work with curls and every hair texture. On very curly hair the straight style can last less, especially with humidity or sweat, and a night wrap helps a lot.

What is the difference between a Dominican blowout and a silk press?

Both leave hair straight and shiny. A Dominican blowout is done mostly with a round brush and blow dryer; a silk press finishes the straightening with a flat iron.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome and we take clients first come, first served. For longer services, like bridal, full color, or extensions, we recommend calling ahead.
