Everything you may want to know before coming in.

Do I need a consultation before bridal or full color?

Yes, it is worth calling first so the plan is clear. For brides, we do a trial for hair and makeup. For full color or correction, we prefer to see the hair or talk through it first.

Do you require a deposit for brides?

To hold the date we ask for a small deposit, and it gets applied to the total later. Every wedding is different, so call us and we will walk you through it.

Do you handle bridal parties and larger events?

Yes. For bridal, reserve your date in advance. We offer a trial and help you plan the beauty schedule for the day.
