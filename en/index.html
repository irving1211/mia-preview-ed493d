Everything you may want to know before coming in.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome and we take clients first come, first served. For longer services, like bridal, full color, or extensions, we recommend calling ahead.

Why are there no exact prices on the website?

Because the final cost changes with hair length, density, design, touch-up needs, or a full transformation. We would rather quote you well once we know what you actually want.

How long does a Dominican blowout last?

With basic care, like a night wrap and keeping it dry, many clients still feel good about it all week, and some stretch it to 10 days.

Do you work with all hair types?

Yes. We specialize in Dominican hair, curls, waves, mixed textures, fine hair, thick hair, and color-treated hair. Each texture gets the right technique.

Do you do nails too?

Yes. We offer polygel, rubber base, resin, acrylic, gel, nail art, and all types of pedicure. You can do hair and nails in the same visit.

What are the best days to come in?

Tuesday through Thursday are the calmest days. Friday and Saturday are busiest. Earlier is better.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash and credit card, Visa, Mastercard, and Amex. If you have a very specific question, call before you come in.

Is there parking?

Yes. There is free street parking and nearby lots on S Broadway. The salon is easy to find.

Clients come from all around

Lawrence, Methuen, Andover, Haverhill, Lowell, and Salem, NH.
